page one · just for you

For the person who makes ordinary moments feel a little less ordinary.

Bas ek chhota sa reminder—teri presence mein normal si baatein bhi thodi zyada achhi lagne lagti hain.

page two · the easy kind

Tere saath baat karna kabhi forced nahi lagta.

Random thoughts, thodi hasi, thodi bakwaas… aur kab time nikal jaata hai, pata hi nahi chalta. That comfort is rare.

page three · a quiet thing

Kuch log bina zyada noise ke apni jagah bana lete hain.

Tu bhi waise hi hai—easy to remember, easy to appreciate, aur honestly, difficult to replace.

page four · whenever needed

Agar kabhi din thoda heavy lage…

Bas message kar dena. Har baar perfect words ya perfect solution zaroori nahi hota—kabhi kabhi bas kisi ka saath enough hota hai.

page five · keep this close

Tu jaisi hai, waisi hi kaafi achhi hai.

Keep being soft, funny, real, aur thodi si unpredictable. The world needs that version of you.
